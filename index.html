// CHAPTER 01: THE ORIGIN STORY

DESIGNER'S DIARY

I believe great design isn't just clean pixels — it's energy, friction, curiosity, and story. I started sketching in margins of math notebooks in middle school, and honestly? I never really stopped.

Over the past 6+ years, I've had the pleasure of partnering with indie beverage companies, Tokyo underground streetwear labels, underground music festivals, and cutting-edge startups. I treat every project like a tailor-made universe: custom lettering, deliberate tactile grit, and personality that cuts through sterile algorithm templates.
